All six examples

Start from the one closest to what you want

Copy one by name and it is yours to change: teamrunner new my_team --from fact_checked

round_robin

The simplest team: three members and no chair, one turn each in name order, and a typed verdict.

multi_agent_debate

The debate on the home page: a chair, with a repeated block of rounds.

fact_checked

The debate with a fact checker that reads the web, and a Python sandbox to check the sums.

hiring_committee

The committee in the big example: facts only one member knows, kept in that member’s own file.

orchestrators_lite

Five engineers review code against planted bugs, sharing one model profile.

reference_check

Checks every reference in a paper: that it exists, that its details are right, and that it says what the citing sentence claims.

teamrunner

Preview release. Until the first announced release, a new version may need changes to teams made with an earlier one.

GPL-3.0-or-later, like R. Your teams, runs and results are yours, under any licence you choose.

Visits are counted with GoatCounter: no cookies, nothing that identifies you.

If you sign up for news, we keep your email only to write to you about TeamRunner. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and a note to hello@teamrunner.ai deletes it.
