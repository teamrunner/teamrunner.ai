Tutorial

Nine steps, from a free rehearsal to your first experiment

Step 1 builds a team from nothing. Every later step starts from your team or one that ships with TeamRunner, and adds one idea. No code: each step is a few words changed in a text file, and a click or a command.

Before you start, install TeamRunner (version 0.0.5 or later). Browser labels below are those of 0.0.5; in 0.0.4, New team, New member, Runs and Check keys and models online were the names for + Team, + Member, Run history and Validate. Follow each step in your browser, on TeamRunner's own page, or in a terminal with any text editor: the switch at each step changes them all. The files you write are the same either way. Step 1 needs no key and costs nothing, and every later step can be rehearsed for free before you spend anything. Real runs incur provider charges that vary with the model, inputs, tools and number of turns; displayed costs are estimates.
