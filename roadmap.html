Roadmap

Planning a study? Here is what comes next

Designed experiments shipped in 0.0.5; the rest of this list is still future design. Managers, whiteboards, subteams and parallel work are still future design. Preview upgrades may require changes to older teams; consult the changelog.

Designed experiments

One file names the versions to compare and how many runs of each. TeamRunner runs them all, seeded, and exports five CSV tables for R, Stata or pandas, with a descriptive summary and 95% intervals.

Who sees what

Seal reviewers from each other, or pass work along a chain where each person hears only the one before. One line each, in a whiteboard file.

Managers

An AI that runs the meeting and decides who speaks next, in place of a fixed script, so script vs. judgment can itself be compared.

Subteams

A folder inside a folder: a subcommittee that deliberates on its own and reports back, so the org chart is the directory tree.

---
name: meeting-format
conditions:
  open:
    from: ../../teams/hiring_committee
  share_first:
    from: ../../teams/hiring_committee
    change:
      chair: file:variants/share_first.md
replications: 25      # 25 runs of each version, 50 in all
seed: 42
limits:
  max_cost_usd: 10
---
Does sharing facts first change the committee's choice?

For experiments/meeting_format/experiment.md, with variants/share_first.md containing the alternate chair and a team in teams/hiring_committee/. Budget checks use estimates and do not cap provider billing. See the experiment tutorial for a complete example.
