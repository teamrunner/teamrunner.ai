Step 9Bigger organizations

follow in

Two larger examples show how far the same few ideas go. orchestrators_lite is five engineers reviewing code with planted bugs, in rounds, one of them writing the report. All five share one model profile in models/, so changing the model there changes it for everyone. Copy it into a team called review, read it back and rehearse it:

+ Team, review, from orchestrators_lite; then Flow diagram and a rehearsal.

teamrunner new review --from orchestrators_lite teamrunner explain review teamrunner run review --rehearse

reference_check checks every reference in a paper: that it exists, that its details are right, and that the cited paper says what the citing sentence claims. Seven members and three toolkeepers, which read the paper and look references up in public catalogues. Copy it into a team called refs, and give it a paper of your own, as a link or a PDF:

+ Team, refs, from reference_check. Then Run…, choose A file under What to give the team, and pick your PDF.

teamrunner new refs --from reference_check teamrunner explain refs teamrunner run refs --file my_paper.pdf

Rehearsing it may end early: the reader's answer, whether it could read the whole paper, is random in a rehearsal, and the team stops when it can't. That is the flow working.
